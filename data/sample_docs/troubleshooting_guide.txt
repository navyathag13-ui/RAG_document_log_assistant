HYDRAULIC SYSTEM TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE
HX-9000 Series  |  Document: TS-HX9000-v3
Revised: 2024-01-10

This guide addresses the most common fault conditions observed in the field.
Work through each issue systematically; do not skip steps.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
ISSUE 1: HYDRAULIC PUMP OVERHEATING
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Symptom: Fluid temperature exceeds 70 °C (WARN-T01) or 80 °C (FAULT-T02).
         Pump may have stopped automatically.

Possible causes and checks:

Step 1 — Verify cooling circuit operation
  a) Check that the heat exchanger cooling fan is running. If the fan motor is
     stopped, inspect the fan thermal fuse (rated 10 A) on the control panel.
     Replace if blown.
  b) Ensure coolant flow through the heat exchanger is unobstructed. The water
     inlet and outlet valves must be fully open.
  c) Clean heat exchanger fins if fouling or debris is visible. Use compressed
     air at ≤ 4 bar. Do not use water jets on the electrical cabinet.

Step 2 — Check fluid viscosity and level
  a) Low fluid level reduces cooling capacity. Top up to MAX mark.
  b) Using the wrong grade of hydraulic oil increases internal leakage and heat
     generation. Required grade: ISO VG 46. Check fluid documentation.

Step 3 — Check pump displacement and loading
  a) If the system is operating at maximum displacement (100%) continuously,
     reduce duty cycle or install a larger reservoir.
  b) Inspect for internal leakage: if case drain flow exceeds 5 L/min, the
     pump may need rebuild or replacement.

Step 4 — Restart after overtemperature fault
  1. Allow fluid to cool to below 50 °C (monitor on SCU display).
  2. Verify and resolve the root cause from Steps 1–3 above.
  3. Press FAULT RESET on the SCU-400 panel.
  4. Restart system using the standard startup procedure (see Manual Section 2).
  5. Monitor temperature for the first 30 minutes of operation.

ISSUE 2: SENSOR COMMUNICATION FAULT (FAULT-S04)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Symptom: SCU displays FAULT-S04. "SENSOR COMM FAULT" on display.
         System may continue running in degraded mode or stop, depending on
         which sensor is affected.

Step 1 — Identify the affected sensor
  The SCU-400 detail screen (press INFO on FAULT-S04 display) shows the
  sensor ID. Common sensors:
    PT-01  Pressure transducer, pump outlet (4–20 mA, 0–400 bar)
    PT-02  Pressure transducer, actuator port A (4–20 mA, 0–400 bar)
    TT-01  Temperature transmitter, reservoir (4–20 mA, 0–120 °C)
    FT-01  Flow transmitter (4–20 mA, 0–150 L/min)

Step 2 — Check wiring and connections
  a) Inspect terminal block TB-3 in the control panel for loose or corroded
     connections. Re-terminate if needed (torque: 0.8 N·m on M3 terminals).
  b) Measure loop current with a clamp meter:
       < 3.8 mA  → open circuit or sensor failure
       > 20.5 mA → short circuit or faulty transmitter
     Normal range: 4 mA (zero scale) to 20 mA (full scale).

Step 3 — Sensor replacement
  a) Isolate the sensor loop by opening CB-3 on the control panel.
  b) Remove the sensor and bench-test against a reference.
  c) Install new sensor; tighten to specified torque (PT-01/PT-02: 25 N·m; TT-01: 15 N·m).
  d) Re-calibrate using SCU calibration menu (MENU > SENSORS > CALIBRATE).

Step 4 — Restart steps after sensor communication fault
  1. Close CB-3 after confirmed sensor replacement or repair.
  2. On the SCU-400, navigate to: MENU > FAULTS > RESET ALL.
  3. Confirm reset with the ENTER key.
  4. The SCU performs a sensor health check (10 seconds). Verify all sensors
     show GREEN status on the diagnostics screen.
  5. Return to STANDBY mode, then follow the standard startup procedure.
  6. Verify live sensor readings are within expected ranges before loading.

ISSUE 3: PRESSURE DROP ALERT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Symptom: System pressure drops below set-point during operation.
         Possible SCU warnings: WARN-P01 (10% below set-point) or
         FAULT-P03 (failed to reach set-point in 60 s).

Step 1 — Check for external leaks
  Walk the full pipe circuit. Any visible oil spray or pooling indicates an
  external leak. Isolate the affected section immediately; do not continue
  operating a leaking system.

Step 2 — Check for internal bypass
  a) If no external leak is found, internal leakage through a relief valve,
     counter-balance valve, or the pump itself is likely.
  b) Check relief valve RV-01 set-point with a calibrated gauge (set-point: 280 bar).
     If relief opens below 260 bar, readjust or replace RV-01.

Step 3 — Inspect pump output
  a) At full displacement, measure pump output pressure and flow.
     Compare to rated values: 350 bar max, 120 L/min at 1500 rpm.
  b) If pump output is significantly below specification, pump wear or
     damage is likely. Refer to Pump Service Manual HS-9000-P.

Step 4 — Check isolating valves
  Confirm all isolating valves in the high-pressure circuit are fully open.
  A partially closed valve can cause localised pressure drop and heat buildup.

Step 5 — Resolve and restart after pressure drop fault
  1. Correct the identified root cause (leak repair, valve adjustment, etc.).
  2. On SCU-400: MENU > FAULTS > CLEAR ACTIVE.
  3. Ramp pressure slowly (< 10 bar/s) to set-point and confirm stability.
  4. Log the incident in the maintenance record with date, fault code, and action taken.

ISSUE 4: PUMP WILL NOT START
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Symptom: PUMP START button pressed; pump motor does not run.

Step 1 — Check power supply
  a) Verify main supply voltage at the main disconnect: expected 380–415 VAC 3-phase.
  b) Check motor protection relay MPR-1: if tripped (indicator red), reset once.
     If it trips again immediately, investigate for motor overcurrent or winding fault.

Step 2 — Check interlock chain
  The pump will not start if any of these interlocks are active:
    - Emergency stop button engaged (pull to reset)
    - Low fluid level switch LSH-1 open (fill reservoir)
    - High-temperature lock-out (fluid must cool to < 60 °C)
    - SCU in FAULT state (clear active faults first)

Step 3 — Control circuit check
  Check 24 VDC control fuse F3 (2 A) in the control panel. Replace if blown.

ISSUE 5: ERRATIC PRESSURE CONTROL
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Symptom: System pressure oscillates ± 15 bar or more around set-point.

Step 1 — Check for air in the system
  Air in the hydraulic circuit causes compressibility-related pressure
  oscillation. Bleed the system by cycling the pressure control potentiometer
  between minimum and maximum pressure 5 times at slow load.

Step 2 — Check SCU PID gain settings
  Navigate to: SCU MENU > CONTROL > PID PARAMETERS.
  Default values:  Kp = 2.5,  Ki = 0.8,  Kd = 0.1.
  If gains have been altered, restore defaults and observe.

Step 3 — Inspect PPCV-12 proportional valve
  A worn or contaminated PPCV-12 spool can cause hunting.
  Flush the valve with clean filtered oil; replace if oscillation persists.
