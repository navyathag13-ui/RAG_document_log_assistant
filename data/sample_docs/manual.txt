SECTION 1: SYSTEM OVERVIEW
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

The HX-9000 hydraulic system is a closed-loop, variable-pressure power unit
designed for precision manufacturing and heavy industrial applications. The
system operates at working pressures from 50 bar to 350 bar and supports
flow rates up to 120 L/min.

Key subsystems:
  - Hydraulic pump (axial-piston, variable displacement)
  - Proportional pressure-control valve (PPCV-12)
  - Servo control unit (SCU-400)
  - Heat exchanger with bypass thermostat
  - Reservoir (150 L, stainless-lined)
  - Return-line filter (10 µm nominal, 16 µm absolute)

The servo control unit monitors system pressure, temperature, and flow rate
at 100 Hz and adjusts pump displacement via the electronic displacement
controller (EDC).

SECTION 2: STARTUP PROCEDURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Follow these steps every time the system is started from a cold state.

2.1  Pre-start checks
  a) Verify reservoir fluid level is between MIN and MAX marks on the sight glass.
  b) Check that the return-line filter indicator is in the GREEN zone.
     If the indicator is RED, replace the filter element before starting.
  c) Confirm all pressure-relief valves are correctly seated (torque: 35 N·m).
  d) Inspect hydraulic hoses for cracks, chafing, or oil seepage. Do not start
     if visible leaks are present.
  e) Ensure the servo control unit (SCU-400) display shows "STANDBY".

2.2  Start sequence
  1. Set main power switch to ON.  SCU-400 performs a 15-second self-test.
  2. Wait for the "SYSTEM OK" message on the SCU display.
     If "FAULT" is displayed, record the fault code and consult Section 5.
  3. Press the PUMP START button. The pump runs at minimum displacement for
     30 seconds to circulate fluid and purge air.
  4. Gradually increase system pressure to operating set-point using the
     pressure-control potentiometer. Do not exceed 10 bar per second ramp rate.
  5. Verify fluid temperature is rising toward operating range (40–55 °C).
  6. When temperature reaches 40 °C, the system is ready for full-load operation.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
SECTION 3: SHUTDOWN PROCEDURE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3.1  Planned shutdown
  1. Reduce system pressure to zero using the pressure-control potentiometer.
  2. Press the PUMP STOP button. Allow the pump to decelerate for 10 seconds.
  3. Set main power switch to OFF.
  4. After shutdown, check fluid level and top up if necessary.

3.2  Emergency shutdown
  Press the red EMERGENCY STOP button on the control panel. This simultaneously
  de-energises the pump motor and opens the dump valve, dropping pressure to zero
  within 2 seconds. After an emergency stop, do not restart until the cause of
  the emergency has been identified and corrected.

SECTION 4: OPERATING LIMITS AND THRESHOLDS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4.1  Pressure
  Normal operating range:   100 – 280 bar
  Maximum allowable:        350 bar (relief valve set-point)
  Low-pressure alarm:        80 bar
  High-pressure alarm:      310 bar

4.2  Temperature
  Cold-start minimum:        15 °C (system will not reach full pressure below this)
  Normal operating range:    40 – 55 °C
  High-temperature warning:  70 °C  → SCU generates WARN-T01
  High-temperature alarm:    80 °C  → SCU generates FAULT-T02, pump stops automatically

4.3  Voltage calibration thresholds (EDC electronics)
  Supply voltage:            24 VDC nominal
  Acceptable range:          22.5 – 26.5 VDC
  Under-voltage alarm:       < 22.0 VDC  → FAULT-V01
  Over-voltage alarm:        > 27.0 VDC  → FAULT-V02
  EDC signal output range:   0 – 10 VDC (represents 0% – 100% pump displacement)
  Zero-point calibration:    Adjust R17 trimmer until output reads 0.00 ± 0.05 VDC
                             with zero displacement command.
  Full-scale calibration:    Adjust R18 trimmer until output reads 10.00 ± 0.05 VDC
                             with 100% displacement command.
  Calibration interval:      Every 2000 operating hours or after EDC replacement.

4.4  Flow rate
  Minimum continuous:       10 L/min
  Maximum rated:           120 L/min
  Low-flow alarm:           < 8 L/min for > 5 seconds → WARN-F01

SECTION 5: FAULT CODES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

FAULT-T02  Fluid temperature exceeded 80 °C. Pump stopped automatically.
           Action: Allow fluid to cool to < 50 °C, then investigate cooling circuit.

FAULT-V01  EDC supply voltage below 22.0 VDC.
           Action: Check 24V power supply output; inspect wiring for resistance faults.

FAULT-V02  EDC supply voltage above 27.0 VDC.
           Action: Check 24V power supply regulation; replace power supply if faulty.

FAULT-P03  Pump pressure failed to reach set-point within 60 seconds.
           Action: Check pump displacement linkage; verify PPCV-12 operation.

FAULT-S04  Sensor communication fault — loss of signal from pressure transducer PT-01.
           Action: Check transducer wiring (4–20 mA loop); replace transducer if open circuit.

WARN-T01   Fluid temperature above 70 °C.
           Action: Verify cooling circuit flow; check heat exchanger for fouling.

WARN-F01   Low flow rate detected.
           Action: Check for partially closed isolating valves; inspect filter for blockage.

SECTION 6: MAINTENANCE SCHEDULE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

  Every 500 hours:
    - Replace return-line filter element
    - Check all hose connections for torque and leaks
    - Verify reservoir fluid level and condition

  Every 1000 hours:
    - Sample hydraulic fluid for contamination analysis (target: ISO 4406 class 16/14/11)
    - Inspect pump shaft seal for leakage
    - Verify pressure-relief valve set-points with calibrated gauge

  Every 2000 hours:
    - Perform full EDC voltage calibration (Section 4.3)
    - Replace heat exchanger gaskets
    - Full pump inspection per Pump Service Manual HS-9000-P

  Annual:
    - Complete fluid change
    - Hydrostatic test of high-pressure lines (1.5 × max working pressure)
